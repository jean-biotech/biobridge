BioBridge is a free, student-led resource. This page explains, plainly, what happens with your information when you use the site.

## What we collect

BioBridge itself does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and does not require an account to read any page.

A few features on the site involve outside services, and each one is scoped to its own purpose:

- **The AI Application Reviewer** (`/application-reviewer/`) sends the job description and resume text you paste in to a Cloudflare Worker, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate feedback. That text is used only to produce your result and is not stored by BioBridge afterward.
- **The Biotech Blueprint checkout** (on the Guides page) is handled entirely by Gumroad. Payment details go directly to Gumroad, not to BioBridge, and are covered by Gumroad's own privacy policy.
- **GitHub Pages**, which hosts this site, may log basic server access data (like IP address and browser type) as part of standard web hosting. BioBridge does not have access to that data beyond what GitHub provides.

## What we don't do

BioBridge does not sell or share your information with third parties, does not run advertising, and does not build profiles of visitors.
